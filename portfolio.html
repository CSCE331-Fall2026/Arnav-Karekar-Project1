Technical Interest and Qualifications

I am interested in the application of machine learning in cyber security. I am currently working as an Undergraduate Researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University, where I study concept drift in Android malware detection. My work there involves building Python pipelines that extract features from the DREBIN and AndroZoo datasets, modeling how apps arrive over time, and benchmarking retraining strategies such as ADWIN and TRANSCENDENT to see which ones hold up best as real-world data shifts underneath a model.

Alongside my research I work as a Student Technician with Texas A&M Technology Services, where I perform frontline triage in Proofpoint Endpoint DLP and investigate anomalous access patterns in Elastic Security and Cisco Identity Intelligence. Previously I interned in cybersecurity with the Arkansas Department of Education, automating Microsoft Defender remediation with PowerShell and running live-fire incident response exercises with Splunk, Security Onion, and Palo Alto Firewall.

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Texas A&M University, graduating in May 2028, and I hold the CompTIA Security+, ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity, and AWS Certified Developer – Associate certifications. What ties all of it together is a simple curiosity: I like understanding how systems break, and I like building the tools that catch it when they do.
